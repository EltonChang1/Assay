# Assay conference-manuscript blueprint

Prepared October 19, 2026, from the [literature review](literature/README.md) and
[academic writing guide](ACADEMIC_WRITING.md). This is an editorial plan. It is
not a submission, a claim of venue readiness, a frozen empirical protocol, or
authorization for another paid run.

## Scientific commitment

**Motivating goal:** test whether metric-grounded, structured evidence review
reduces incorrect quantitative releases relative to ordinary-summary review at
the same combined allowed oversight resources while preserving correct required
answers.

**Current supported scope:** a narrow synthetic settled-revenue system with
separate obligations, concrete semantic counterexamples, selected publication
invariants and an unsuccessful analyst-feasibility pilot. No D/E effect has been
measured. Choose a future paper's contribution type after establishing a
meaningful completed result, not from the desired title or venue reputation.

## Main-paper plan

The allocation below totals **9 content pages**. It is a planning example for a
9-page venue, not a mandate or the ICML 2026 limit. References and permitted
appendices follow the exact venue's rules. For an 8-page venue, redesign around
the central insight; do not reduce type size or omit essential evidence. For SIGMOD 2027 research or PVLDB v20 regular/EA&B, plan within the verified
12-page allowance and actual appendix policy; other categories differ.

| Section | Editorial allowance | What the reader should learn |
|---|---:|---|
| Title, abstract and introduction | 1.25 pages | Goal, concrete authentic-but-wrong example, nearest gap, contribution and strongest completed evidence |
| Problem, assumptions and closest work | 1.00 | Metric, claim/release unit, adversary capabilities, trusted boundary and distinction from SQL verification/provenance/control |
| Technical insight and derivation | 1.00 | Why the obligations differ; what property the new mechanism is meant to add; explicit assumptions and boundary cases |
| Method and publication protocol | 1.50 | Supported checking language, reviewer views, algorithm/state transitions, revision/source binding and resource admission |
| Evaluation design | 1.00 | Research questions, task splits, independent truth, baselines, budgets, complete assignment and failure handling |
| Completed results and analysis | 2.00 | Safety/coverage/cost, paired comparisons and uncertainty, mechanism ablations, strongest failures and external-validity tests |
| Related-work synthesis and limitations | 1.00 | Precise difference from the closest literature and limits on inference |
| Conclusion | 0.25 | Direct answer to the opening question and supported transferable insight |

Discussion of essential close prior work belongs early even if a fuller synthesis
appears later. Exact order can change with the scientific result. The main paper
must remain understandable without reading an appendix.

## Opening paragraph appropriate to the present evidence

> A quantitative claim can cite an authentic SQL result and still answer the wrong
> question. For example, joining an order to its line items repeats the order's
> amount even when every returned cell and execution receipt is genuine. This
> work asks which analytical and authorization obligations must be established
> before an AI analyst's answer is released. We instantiate those obligations for
> a narrow settled-revenue task and examine their mechanism behavior and limits.

That opening is an illustration, not a substitute for a novelty comparison. If a
future completed experiment becomes the primary contribution, rewrite the
opening around its tested hypothesis and principal observation.

## Proposed figures and tables

| Artifact | Scientific question | Current availability |
|---|---|---|
| One small authentic-but-wrong example | Why do receipts and cell binding not settle correctness? | Saved numerical fixtures; available |
| Protocol diagram with trust and revision boundaries | What additional release property does the system enforce? | Can be derived from implementation; label assumptions |
| Closest-work matrix | What is added beyond verification, provenance, monitoring and abstention? | Literature reviewed; feature claims need careful inspection |
| Supported/unsupported semantic-family table | What can the checker establish, reject or leave unknown? | Development tests exist; broader systematic coverage is future work |
| Main paired risk/coverage/cost table | Does D/E differ under the declared constraints? | Unavailable: no review reached |
| Matched operating-point or risk/coverage plot | Is apparent safety explained by lower useful output? | Future measurement; include all-withhold reference |
| Ablation/failure table | Which component or assumption explains the result? | Mechanism counterexamples exist; model-effect ablations do not |

Future artifacts must not appear as empty or invented result tables in a
submitted paper. No proposed model outcome should be illustrated as if measured.

## What moves out of the main argument

Keep full schemas, source-location maps, exhaustive SQL cases, environment
digests, commands, long prompts, all per-case outcomes, full proofs beyond the
main insight, and accounting/recovery details in allowed appendices/artifacts.
Retain the information necessary to interpret the main claim in the main text.
For PVLDB v20, appendices count within its content allowance; use external
artifacts according to its actual policies rather than assuming unlimited space.

The full self-contained canonical report remains useful for audits. A short
submission should not become an untraceable second account of what was measured.

## Evidence needed before the intended empirical claim

1. Demonstrate that legitimate analysts can reach valid submissions on the actual
   worker and representative previews. Preserve the old failed pilot.
2. Complete the required combined-budget, recovery and evaluator-information
   boundaries; independently validate task and oracle contracts.
3. Build development/calibration/confirmation partitions that separate structures,
   schema and attack families. Record exposure and avoid tuning on confirmation.
4. Choose compatible strong baselines from the reviewed literature; freeze
   implementations, prompts, models, allowed information, resource limits and
   analysis before outcomes.
5. Establish a feasible precision plan and separately authorized spending plan,
   preserving cumulative charges. The old sample-count arithmetic is insufficient.
6. Complete assigned episodes and charge accounting. Score exact released claims
   independently, report useful coverage and failures, and reproduce every result
   table from saved records.

These steps describe work needed for a particular empirical contribution. They
are not a rule that a meaningful theory or systems paper must run that identical
study. A different scientific story needs its own complete supporting evidence.

## Submission package after a venue is chosen

Use the official, correct-year style and author/reviewer instructions; verify
submission versus final limits; remove identifying information only for an
anonymous track; provide required impact/ethics/checklist/AI-use statements;
validate every reference's version and publication status; package reproducible
artifacts without credentials or active holdouts; and inspect the final PDF.
Keep author responsibility and contribution attribution accurate. Submit or
publish only through the user's selected release process.
