# Conference requirements and academic writing guidance for Assay

**Checked: October 19, 2026. Scope:** official main/research-track policies for the latest cycles verified below. This is a research and planning document, not a submission or a prediction of acceptance. Requirements can change; recheck the chosen venue's live instructions before preparing a submission. Workshop, position, dataset, demo, industry, and main tracks have different rules.

## 1. What the venue evaluates

Strong formatting makes a paper eligible for consideration. The scientific case must still show a precise problem, an original contribution in relation to the closest literature, appropriate methods, and evidence that supports the actual claims.

| Verified venue/cycle | Official review emphasis | Consequence for Assay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NeurIPS 2026, main track | Quality, clarity, significance, and originality; contribution-specific guidance. Theory requires correct claims and appropriate assumptions. Concept & Feasibility retains rigorous support and a high originality/significance bar. Negative Results requires explanatory analysis and an informative, surprising result, rather than an experiment merely failing. [Reviewer guidance](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuidelines) | A six-call pilot that never submits a claim is a feasibility failure. It is not by itself evidence that structured review helps or fails, and does not automatically qualify as a significant negative result. |
| ICML 2026, main track | Soundness, presentation, significance, and originality are evaluated separately. Claims must follow from suitable theory or well-designed experiments; modest impact does not excuse poor soundness. Originality can arise from new insight or a justified combination of existing techniques. [Reviewer instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerInstructions) | Explain exactly what execution receipts, analytical contracts, revision binding, and equal-budget review add beyond their closest predecessors. A working implementation alone does not establish the empirical safety hypothesis. |
| ICLR 2027, conference | Reviewers ask which specific problem is addressed, whether the approach is motivated and situated in prior work, whether rigorous results support the claims, and whether the work contributes valuable new knowledge. State-of-the-art scores are not required. [Reviewer guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/ReviewerGuidelines) | A bounded systems contribution needs convincing new knowledge useful to the ML community; length and mathematical notation do not substitute for that knowledge. |
| SIGMOD 2027, research | Substantial novelty and contribution to data management; topics include provenance, quality, analytics, and systems for AI. E&A contributes analytical insight; DI&DS applications require deployment experience. No separate numerical scoring rubric was verified. [Research call](https://2027.sigmod.org/calls_papers_sigmod_research.shtml) | A database-facing version could emphasize semantic assurance and controlled publication for analytical queries. The current synthetic prototype should not be presented as a deployed DI&DS application. |
| PVLDB volume 20 / VLDB 2027, research | Scope requires a core data-management challenge, substantive engagement with database literature, and evaluation grounded in data-management applications. Merely mentioning SQL or citing a previous database paper is insufficient. [Submission guidelines](https://www.vldb.org/2027/submission-guidelines.html) | Demonstrate the advancement over provenance, query validation, semantic layers, and workflow systems; assess supported/unsupported query shapes and realistic analytical workloads. |

**Interpretation for this repository:** the strongest currently supported story is the separation of execution authenticity, quantitative binding, supported metric semantics, and current-revision release authority, with concrete counterexamples and carefully bounded implementation results. A claim that the protocol reduces model-induced incorrect releases requires new completed evidence. This assessment follows the distinction already made in `research/paper.md`; it is not an official venue decision.

## 2. Exact format differences

The limits below refer to **initial submission** unless explicitly stated otherwise. Figures, tables, equations, and essential methods belong inside the content allowance. Do not infer that another venue permits unlimited appendices simply because the ML venues do.

| Venue and track | Main-content allowance and exclusions | Template and review identity | Accepted/final version |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NeurIPS 2026 main | 9 content pages; references, optional technical appendices, and mandatory checklist are outside that limit. One PDF, up to 50 MB. | Official 2026 LaTeX style only. Double blind extends to supplements and linked code. Omit acknowledgments for review. | One extra content page. [Main-track handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook) |
| ICML 2026 main | 8 main pages; unlimited references, impact statement, and appendices in the same PDF. Submission file at most 50 MB. | Official 2026 LaTeX style; anonymous paper and supplements. | 9 main pages; 20 MB final PDF. [Author instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorInstructions), [call](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers) |
| ICLR 2027 conference | 9 initial main pages; unlimited references and appendices after them. Appendix reading is optional for reviewers. | Official 2027 LaTeX style; anonymous main text and supplements. | 10 main pages for discussion/rebuttal and final. The same page's later FAQ/final wording inconsistently calls 10 identical to submission; use the explicit 9-page initial rule. [Author guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines) |
| SIGMOD 2027 research | 12 pages plus unlimited citations; 10 MB PDF. Short separate review-only appendix; self-contained main paper. | Letter, two-column ACM `sigconf`; double anonymity. | 13 pages excluding references, no appendix; convert to PACMMOD after acceptance. [Research call](https://2027.sigmod.org/calls_papers_sigmod_research.shtml) |
| PVLDB v20 regular research or EA&B | 12 pages excluding references; appendices and acknowledgments count inside the 12. SDS is 8 and Vision 6; these are different categories. | Single blind: include author names and affiliations. [Submission guidelines](https://www.vldb.org/2027/submission-guidelines.html) | Use the current official PVLDB template already for review; do not omit mandatory publication/reference/artifact blocks. The checked instructions do not grant an extra accepted-paper page. [Formatting guidelines](https://www.vldb.org/2027/formatting-guidelines.html), [official template](https://github.com/vldbproceedings/VLDB-Template) |

For ML venues, the official style determines the typography and column structure. Do not manually shrink fonts, margins, equations, or figures to fit. A polished 24-page single-column report is not automatically an 8- or 9-page conference submission. The report can remain the complete canonical technical document while a venue version presents one self-contained central argument and references its allowable supporting detail.

## 3. Supplements, reproducibility, ethics, and AI use

| Venue | Verified requirements and recommendations |
| --- | --- |
| NeurIPS 2026 main | Complete the required paper checklist. A separately named broader-impact section is not mandatory, but consider potential negative impacts. Anonymous code/data may be supplied in one ZIP up to 100 MB. Document dependencies and explain non-executable components. Describe important nonstandard use of agents/LLMs in research methods; authors remain responsible for claims, figures, and verified references. [Handbook](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook) |
| ICML 2026 main | Include the required impact statement before references; it is outside the main-page limit. Authors are responsible for AI-assisted content; LLMs cannot be authors. [Call](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers) Anonymous code can be uploaded or linked, with a repository branch frozen after the deadline. The final-paper workflow adds a lay summary, publication forms, and an automatic format check. [Author instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorInstructions) |
| ICLR 2027 | AI-use statement required, outside the page limit; ethics statement recommended when relevant (at most one page, outside the limit), reproducibility paragraph strongly encouraged and outside the limit. Code is encouraged, not mandatory. [Author guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines) The AI policy specifically requires disclosure of contributions such as synthetic-data generation, hypothesis/method design, mathematical claims/proofs, implementation of methods, and interpretation. Human authors bear responsibility. [AI policy](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AIPolicyForAuthors) |
| SIGMOD 2027 research | Anonymous artifact link or explanation expected by deadline; availability generally optional for acceptance. Benchmark/dataset E&A has stronger expectations. ACM human-participant, authorship, and AI policies apply. [Research call](https://2027.sigmod.org/calls_papers_sigmod_research.shtml) Artifact availability, evaluated reusability, and independently reproduced results are distinct badges; none should be claimed just because tests pass. [Availability and Reproducibility Initiative](https://reproducibility.sigmod.org/) |
| PVLDB v20 | Submit the artifacts used for results, with permanent accessible hosting and usable instructions, or an acceptable substantive explanation. EA&B has no artifact exemption: the initial submission needs the complete reproducibility package and the experiments must enter reproducibility evaluation. [Submission guidelines](https://www.vldb.org/2027/submission-guidelines.html) |

For Assay, provide a claim-to-artifact index: every table/figure points to saved evidence, its version, and a deterministic analysis command. Separate rerunning analysis on saved paid-model outputs from making fresh API calls. Include the complete assignment/attempt ledger and unresolved outcomes. Supply a meaningful fresh-environment container check when possible, and report environment limitations accurately. Release credentials or mutable account state neither as artifacts nor as prompt context. These are project-specific implementation recommendations, not additional rules attributed to a conference.

Do not put instructions for automated reviewers into a manuscript. Assay can describe and quote attack inputs as labeled experimental data where scientifically necessary; the paper itself must not attempt to influence the review process through hidden or adversarial instructions.

## 5. Primary writing guidance and how to apply it

These are authors' writing recommendations, not binding conference formats.

1. **Simon Peyton Jones, _How to Write a Great Research Paper_.** The primary slides advocate developing one clear idea through writing, making contributions explicit, explaining with examples before excessive abstraction, and giving fair credit. For Assay: use the duplicated-order example to expose the need for separate obligations, then introduce formal objects that solve exactly that problem. [Microsoft Research resource](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-program/write-great-research-paper/), [author's slides](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/How-to-write-a-great-research-paper.pdf)
2. **Jennifer Widom, _Tips for Writing Technical Papers_ (2006, revised presentation notes).** Her introduction framework covers the problem, importance, difficulty, prior-work gap, and approach/results, with explicit limitations and contributions. She recommends defining notation before use, checking bibliography details, and keeping the main argument understandable without appendices. For Assay: distinguish the completed systems question from the unanswered comparison in the opening; place the concrete novel mechanism and evidence early. [Author's Stanford notes](https://cs.stanford.edu/people/widom/paper-writing.html)
3. **Markus Püschel, _A Descriptive Title, Not Too General, Not Too Long_ (teaching guide).** The guide connects motivation, prior work, method, and experimental setup, and requires each plot to answer an explicit question. For Assay: label a figure as mechanism behavior, feasibility, or comparative outcome; explain its denominator, provenance, and inference immediately. [Author's CMU guide](https://users.ece.cmu.edu/~pueschel/teaching/18-799B-CMU-spring05/material/conference.pdf)

An effective Assay conference narrative would be:

1. **Opening and abstract:** one concrete quantitative-answer failure; one bounded question; contribution and strongest completed result; the key limitation.
2. **Problem and closest work:** define the analytical contract and adversary/trust boundary; establish what provenance, SQL validation, proof-carrying results, and AI-control protocols already solve.
3. **First-principles analysis:** state assumptions, derive why authentic execution and claim binding do not imply semantic correctness, then state the exact conditional invariant enforced by the release protocol.
4. **Method:** explain the minimal architecture, algorithms, revision state machine, supported query class, and conservative unknown cases needed to establish that invariant.
5. **Evaluation:** align each research question with independently derived truth, baselines, interventions, completed outcomes, uncertainty, coverage, resource accounting, and failure analysis. Keep the prospective protocol visibly distinct.
6. **Discussion and conclusion:** explain the transferable insight, the supported scope, unresolved failure paths, and which further observation could falsify the intended empirical benefit.

That narrative is a project-specific synthesis of the review standards and writing guides. It does not require more mathematical notation than the argument needs. New propositions need proofs or explicit counterexamples; empirical claims need completed measurements. No writing or format change can convert an unexecuted confirmatory protocol into a result.
