# Research deliverable and evidence discipline

The primary deliverable is one self-contained, highly technical research paper:
`research/paper.md`, rendered as `output/pdf/assay-technical-report.pdf`.
Methods, experiment execution, analysis, and implementation should serve that
paper's explicit research question. Keep the paper current during development;
the final writing phase is not the first time methods are documented.

- Distinguish implemented behavior, source audits, deterministic development
  regressions, paid-model pilot observations, and held-out confirmation.
- Before model experiments, freeze assignments, hypotheses/outcomes, prompts,
  source/data/check/scorer versions, model settings, budgets, and failure rules.
  Preserve every assignment, attempt, dispatch, charge, and exact released output.
- Compare D/E with equal combined allowed oversight resources, including D's
  summary generation and all correction reviews. Enforce limits before dispatch.
- Derive evaluation truth independently of the runtime verifier. Do not tune on
  confirmation answers or describe new numerical seeds as structural holdouts.
- Report safety with required-answer coverage, uncertainty, failures, and cost.
  Missing outcomes are not evidence of safe withholding. Preserve negative and
  inconclusive findings; a positive result is not required for completion.
- Reproduce numerical claims from saved versioned evidence. Tests and scripted
  trajectories establish mechanism behavior, not comparative model safety.
- User authorized use of their existing OpenAI API account on October 18, 2026,
  with an estimated remaining balance near USD 20. The current development pilot
  uses a conservative USD 15 hard cap; this is not a recurring authorization to
  spend another USD 15 per rerun. Preserve cumulative spending across resumes.
- Keep credentials outside source, prompts, worker mounts, logs, and reports.
  Persist mutable runtime state outside this OneDrive checkout.

The user's personal ATS, quiet LinkedIn updates, and evidence-based career
writing preferences still apply when those tasks arise; this project guidance
does not authorize publishing, announcements, messaging, or profile edits.

## Academic manuscript guidance

For manuscript revisions, consult `research/ACADEMIC_WRITING.md`, the primary-source
notes under `research/literature/`, and `research/SUBMISSION_OUTLINE.md`. These
preserve the October 19, 2026 literature and conference-format review. Refresh
closest-work/version checks and the exact selected venue's official requirements
before submission; the saved rules are dated, not universal future rules.

Lead with a precise question and a supported contribution. Distinguish established
foundations, implementation work, explanatory derivations and original findings.
Do not inflate novelty, add equations for appearance, or turn a failed feasibility
pilot into comparative safety evidence. Keep required-answer coverage, failures,
uncertainty and total oversight resources alongside safety claims. Use the venue's
official template for a submission version and preserve the complete canonical
report. Writing guidance does not authorize paid experiments or publication.
